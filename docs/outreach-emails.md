# Partner outreach drafts

Written 2026-08-09, for sending from `hello@korido.app`.

Two rules these follow, because both protect the thing that makes Korido worth
partnering with:

1. **No invented traffic.** We launched this week and have none. Saying so costs
   nothing and stops the conversation starting on a false footing.
2. **Two asks, and the second matters more.** A tracking link earns commission.
   *Rate access* is what lets us show a provider accurately — and for the ones
   we currently exclude, it is the only route back onto the board.

## Verified contact addresses

Checked 2026-08-09 against each provider's own legal, contact and privacy pages.
**None of them publishes a `partnerships@` address — do not invent one.** A
bounce is a wasted first impression, and guessed addresses at a money company
get filtered.

| Provider | Published | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Wise** | `partnerwise@wise.com` | Apply via Partnerize first, then email with questions |
| **LemFi** | `press@lemfi.com`, `support@lemfi.com`, `complaints@lemfi.com` | **press@** — comms routes business enquiries; support answers as customer service |
| **Sendwave** | `help@sendwave.com` | Skip it — apply through FlexOffers, where they are listed |
| **Taptap Send** | none | LinkedIn, or in-app support asking to be routed to partnerships |
| **Xoom** | none | PayPal partner structure, still to be researched |

For the two with no address, LinkedIn works better than a cold inbox anyway:
search the company plus "partnerships", "growth" or "affiliate", and paste the
email below into a connection note.

**Before sending:** re-check the board and update the figures. Rates move
daily — LemFi was second yesterday. A wrong number in the first paragraph of an
email about accuracy would be the worst possible opener.

## 2. Providers we exclude — Remitly, WorldRemit, Ria

This one is stronger than it looks. It tells them they are absent from a
comparison their competitors appear in, and exactly how to fix it.

> **Subject:** Korido — we'd like to include Remitly but can't read your pricing
>
> Hello,
>
> I run Korido (korido.app), a money transfer comparison for the US→Nigeria,
> Ghana and Kenya corridors. We quote providers live and rank by the amount that
> actually reaches the recipient.
>
> We currently compare Wise, LemFi, Sendwave, Taptap Send and Xoom. Remitly is
> not on the board, and I'd like to change that.
>
> The reason is simply that we can't obtain your prices in a way we're
> comfortable with. Your calculator API returns 429 to unrecognised clients, and
> we won't work around that — misrepresenting who we are isn't a way we want to
> do business. So rather than estimate your rates, we leave you out and tell
> visitors plainly that a provider we don't cover may beat our top result.
>
> If there's a partner feed, sandbox, or even a scheduled CSV we could use, we'd
> add you promptly and treat your pricing exactly as we treat everyone's. An
> affiliate arrangement would be welcome too, but the pricing access is the part
> that matters — we won't list a provider we can't quote accurately.
>
> Best,
> Peter

Adapt the middle paragraph per provider:

- **Remitly** — "your calculator API returns 429 to unrecognised clients"
- **WorldRemit** — "your calculator sits behind a bot challenge"
- **Ria** — "your pricing host closes the connection to non-browser clients"
